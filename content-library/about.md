# About

I'm Reinier, a data engineer and naturalist based in the Netherlands. I built this site to keep track of what I read, what I think about it, and what I find growing in the ground. The [[Books/index|Library]] is a working record of ideas from books I've read across biology, mathematics, philosophy, and related fields. The [[Forest/index|Forest]] is a field guide to the plants and fungi I actually encounter.
